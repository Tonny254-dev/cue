Everything you need to control water

From real-time flow monitoring to automatic leak response, AquaPulse brings your water system into one clear view.

Real-Time Flow Monitoring

Watch live water flow rates (L/min) and consumption as readings arrive from your flow sensor.

Automatic Leak Detection

Get alerted when abnormal flow lasts too long, with severity levels from low to critical.

Valve Control

Open or close the valve manually, or let the system respond automatically to critical leaks.

Consumption Insights

Understand daily, weekly and monthly usage with clear charts and period comparisons.

Smart Notifications

Leaks, abnormal flow, valve issues and disconnections — all surfaced in one notification center.

Reports & Exports

Generate daily, weekly and monthly reports, then export to CSV or print a clean copy.

How AquaPulse works

A simple pipeline from sensor to dashboard.

1. Sense

A water flow sensor measures flow rate.

2. Send

An ESP32 microcontroller sends readings to the backend.

3. Analyze

The backend detects leaks and stores history.

4. Monitor

You monitor and control everything from this dashboard.
