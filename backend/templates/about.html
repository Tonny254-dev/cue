About CUE

CUE monitors any liquid that flows through pipes — not water alone. One inlet/outlet differential algorithm, one live dashboard, and one valve response path for every fluid in the line.

Who we are

CUE is a Kenyan-built liquid transport platform: Arduino/IoT sensing, a live operator dashboard, leak detection, SMS alerts, and automated valve control. Our brand mark — CUE — stands for clear, usable liquid intelligence for utilities, farms, depots, plants, and industry.

From the pipe to the phone, CUE turns silent product loss into visible action: live L/min readings, locus estimates for repairs, and fast shut-off when a leak is confirmed. The physics of the line matter — not the brand of the liquid. If it flows in a pipe, CUE is built to watch it.

Problem

Undetected leaks and late manual response waste whatever product is in the pipe — and raise costs. Many sites have no affordable way to compare inlet vs outlet flow or shut a line automatically when balance breaks.

Mission

Deliver an affordable, reliable monitoring and control system that detects leaks early on any liquid line, prevents wastage, and makes pipeline stewardship practical for everyday operators.

Vision

Accessible IoT for liquid logistics — municipal water, oil & fuel, chemicals, and process fluids — so operators protect every litre that moves through the pipe with smart, local infrastructure.

One algorithm. Many fluids.

CUE compares inlet and outlet flow (S1/S2), learns the healthy pattern for that line, and flags imbalance. Sensor hardware must suit the fluid (pressure, chemistry, viscosity); the software model stays the same.

Guarantee — when CUE works on every liquid

The differential algorithm (inlet − outlet) is the same for every fluid. We guarantee leak detection behaviour for single-phase pipe liquids when all of these hold:

  • Both S1 and S2 sensors are compatible with the fluid (material + meter type)
  • Both ends share a correct K-factor (pulses per litre) for that meter/fluid
  • Inlet flow is above the fluid’s arm threshold (so dry-pipe noise cannot trip a leak)
  • The line is liquid-filled — not multiphase with free gas

Explicitly out of guarantee (system will refuse or warn, not “pretend”):

  • Multiphase / aerated lines (need specialised multiphase meters)
  • YF-S201 water meters on fuel or aggressive chemicals
  • One sensor offline (reported as a sensor fault, not a false leak)
  • Uncalibrated K-factor (absolute L/min wrong; matched ends still see relative loss)
